Our Educational Philosophy

At Gimnasio Campestre Marcello IAFrancesco, we believe that education is more than just academic performance. It is about fostering a lifelong love for learning through integrated experiences that engage the head, heart, and hands.

🍃 Montessori Inspiration

We provide prepared environments that allow students to lead their own discovery journey, following the scientific principles of Maria Montessori to support natural development.

🤝 Values & Integrity

Every lesson is infused with values like respect, honesty, and empathy. We nurture the character of our students as much as their intellect.

🌳 Campestre Environment

Our campus in Cajicá offers wide open spaces where students connect with nature every day, facilitating outdoor learning and ecological awareness.
